Why MINI?

A format designed for people who are curious, busy, and want to understand more without wasting time.

01 — INSIGHTFUL

Big ideas, made simple.

Complex subjects are broken down into the core idea so you can actually understand what matters.

02 — FOCUSED

Respecting your time.

No long introductions and no unnecessary filler. The episode gets to the idea quickly.

03 — USEFUL

Leave with something.

The goal is not just to hear information — it is to finish with a clearer way of thinking about the topic.

The idea behind it

There are fascinating ideas everywhere — finance, psychology, technology, society, and everyday life. MINI Podcast takes those ideas and asks one question: how can this be explained clearly in just a few minutes?

That is the MINI way: less time, more understanding.
